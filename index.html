❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers.

Is NezukoHelper free?

Yes! NezukoHelper is completely free to add and use in your server.

How do I see all the commands?

Just type /help in any channel where NezukoHelper is present. You will get the full command list.

How does the AI chat work?

Use /ask followed by your question, mention @NezukoHelper in a message, right inside Discord. NezukoHelper understands any language but always answers in English.

How do giveaways work?

An admin runs /giveaway, sets the prize and duration (like 30m, 2h or 1d). Members press the Enter button, and NezukoHelper automatically announces the winner when time runs out.

How do I give new members a role automatically?

An admin uses /autorole_bot_add and selects a role. Every new member will receive it automatically on joining.

I found a problem. What should I do?

Use the /report command inside your server or contact the bot owner. Please never share passwords, OTPs or tokens with anyone.
